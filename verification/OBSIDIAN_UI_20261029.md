# TIMEVAULT — obsidian and smoked amethyst

The presentation returns to a mysterious dark material palette: near-black and smoked purple backgrounds, static fine grain, a restrained diagonal grazing light, silver-violet typography and muted violet controls. BUY BACK retains a distinct cold-blue accent.

The desktop cover vessel is approximately 18% larger (scale 0.34 → 0.40); its vertical placement and hit area follow the larger object. The title is slightly smaller to balance the composition. Narrow-screen vessel scale is preserved. Title illumination, vessel drag/hold/reverse/reset, moving clock hands and all existing content remain in place.

NOTICE, GATHER and RETURN use the same near-black, silver and lavender palette before and after camera activation. Camera tracking, physical left/right hand identity, gesture timing, particle motion and chapter actions are unchanged. The hand worker, vision bundle, model and WASM assets remain identical to the preceding release.
